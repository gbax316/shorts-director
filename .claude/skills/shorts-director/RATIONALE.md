# Why shorts-director is shaped this way

Not loaded by the skill. For whoever edits it next.

**Source.** Built on 1 November 2026 through os-skillify (from-content, hybrid lens) from the brief at Personal OS `os-outputs/shorts-director/BRIEF.md`, the fresh-eyes review of the same day, two proving runs made by hand (Akpos and Segs episode 2, Qodeup mini documentary 1), `LESSONS.md` 1 to 22, the two series bibles, and Hasan Aboul Hasan's make-ai-short skill. Single lane rather than dual-lane: the proving runs are the evidence, and a second blind draft could not add evidence the runs did not have.

**One skill with modes, project-scoped.** The fresh-eyes review named weight as the main risk of a director over several engines. One router with seven modes keeps the skill surface to a single entry, and living in this repo's `.claude/skills/` keeps it off the owner's global list.

**The writers' room comes first.** The installed creator's grammar is for explainers (payoff at frame 0, then a loop). Skits and documentaries need their own, and in both runs the premise stage was where the owner's direction changed the film for free.

**Gates are where the owner's eye goes; checks are where it does not.** Both runs showed the split: the owner redirected premises, voices and focus, while every visual fault was caught by the frame scan before hand-over. The skill keeps those two jobs separate on purpose.

**Identity in config.** The owner wants to share the repo and sell what it makes. Brand, people, links, voices and prices live in `director.config.json`; the skill carries none of them inline, apart from the proving runs it cites as worked examples.

**Additive.** Formats keep coming (the owner already asked for range beyond stick figures), so the skill carries the self-extending pattern for formats, with `os-skillify` writing a new format reference after its proving run.

**The improve mode.** The owner asked for a skill that improves as it is used. The brief's loop table (trigger, where the lesson lands, who approves, the way back) is implemented as a mode that runs after every short, plus `RUNS.md` as the record it scores against. An owner's correction about how the skill works counts as a second occurrence, because two of the session's corrections (markdown review in the side pane, hooks built from the course) were repeats the owner had already made once.

**Left out of v1, on purpose.** Automated posting and analytics (read by hand first), thumbnails and the 16:9 and 4:5 cuts (in the brief's finish mode but not yet proved by a run), the cold-viewer review by another model family (in the evals, not yet run). Each should land through the improve mode once a run proves it.

**Open.** Audience numbers do not exist yet, so the outcomes table's viewer rows have no baseline, and "better than the installed creator" remains a claim about quality, not about views.
