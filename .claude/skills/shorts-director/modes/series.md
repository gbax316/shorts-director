# Series

Every channel has a bible, even one without a cast. It is the file every episode reads before writing a line and writes back to after it ships, so the tenth episode knows what the first nine settled.

Write it at `ai-shorts/series/<series>/BIBLE.md` from evidence: the owner's dictation, the episodes or prototypes that already exist, the offer's own pages. The bibles in this repo are the working examples to copy the shape from: `ai-shorts/series/akpos-segs/BIBLE.md` for a recurring cast, `ai-shorts/series/qodeup-docs/BIBLE.md` for a channel with no cast.

A bible for a cast covers who each character is and how they talk, the friendship rules (who wins, who never gets mean), the catchphrases and gags with the episode that introduced each, the world and its places, the sound palette, the production locks (reference images, voice ids and settings, mouth settings), and an episode table with status. A bible for a channel covers the grammar, who speaks and in what person, the look (including the income-level and contrast rules), kinetic type, the sound palette, how people are shown, and a film table.

Two habits keep it useful. First, it records decisions with their date and who made them, because a rule with no history gets argued again. Second, each finished episode writes back what it settled in the same session: a new catchphrase, a narrator confirmed, a status changed. An episode that ships without updating the bible leaves the next one guessing.

Identity a bible names (a lead facilitator, a brand, a link) should match `director.config.json`; when they disagree, the config wins and the bible gets corrected.
