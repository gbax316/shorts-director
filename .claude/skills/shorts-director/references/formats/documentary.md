# Format: mini documentary

A narrator over photoreal scenes, with every figure drawn as kinetic type and its source on screen. Proved by Qodeup mini documentary 1, "The new builders", finished on 1 November 2026. The working files are `ai-shorts/doc-1/` and the bible at `ai-shorts/series/qodeup-docs/BIBLE.md`.

## Grammar

Question, evidence, turn. The question lands at frame 0 over a person doing the thing the film is about. The evidence is two to four sourced figures, each on its own card over a scene that makes it physical (a night map lighting up laptop by laptop). The turn shows what opens up (doors onto a site, an app, a product of your own) and names who offers the way in. A closing line on the night ground, then the end card.

## Who speaks

The narrator speaks for the company ("we") and names its people by role (in documentary 1, the lead facilitator). An AI voice does not speak as a real person in the first person. Claims about the company come from the company's own site; claims about the world pass the fact gate (`../facts.md`) with the source under every figure.

## Look

Photoreal, modern, warm light, bright sharp contrast. People across income levels (a banker's high-rise flat and a shared desk in the same film) and nothing run down. People are generated and shown as "a builder like you", never as named real people, and a returning person is generated from the first still as reference. Place and time tags in code ("LEKKI, 9:40 PM"). Screens are left blank in the stills and drawn in code, pinned with a perspective transform.

## Default render paths

Generated stills that move with `Parallax` (2.5D depth from `tools/parallax_layers.py`) for most beats, a slow push only where the cut-out fails (a crowd, a skyline); figures, tags, maps, screens and doors drawn in code; one or two generated clips where a person's action is the beat (typing, pressing publish). A clip shorter than its scene plays first and the scene holds on its last frame.

## Voice

One narrator for the channel, chosen from three auditions (Ifeoma Odumodu for Qodeup, confirmed by the owner). Narration length is timed as the writers' room says (`../../modes/writers-room.md`); a narrator who pauses at commas needs fewer words, and anything already on a card can leave the narration.

## What a documentary costs

Documentary 1: nine stills (162 credits) and two clips at 720p (369 credits), the narration on ElevenLabs, the bed and cues from the library. Its rounds were direction (the audience, then the company focus), which a sharper buyer statement in the premises would have caught earlier.
