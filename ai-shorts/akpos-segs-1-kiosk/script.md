# Akpos and Segs 1: Segs's kiosk

Akpos and Segs are close friends. Segs runs a small kiosk downtown. Akpos stops by, finds him
still behind the same counter, and tells him people have to see him online to buy from him.
Segs says he is too old to sit down and learn coding. Akpos tells him about Orezi's training.
9:16, generated pixels (make-ai-short), characters locked in `ai-shorts/akpos` and
`ai-shorts/segs`.

## The dialogue

| # | Who | Line | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Segs | Akpos, how far now? | Behind his kiosk counter, waves as Akpos walks up. |
| 2 | Akpos | I'm fine. Segs, you're still inside this small kiosk? | Leans on the counter, looks the kiosk up and down. |
| 3 | Akpos | You know digital marketing is taking over these days. People need to see you to buy from you. You will not go and get a website for yourself? | Holds up his phone to Segs, taps the screen. |

## The setting

A small kiosk on a busy downtown street: freshly painted, bright, neatly stocked (drinks, snacks,
recharge cards), a lively street behind. It reads as a going business that just has no online
presence, never as poverty. Same flat 2D style and paper backdrop tones as the character sheets.

This one names the dates, so it stops being usable after 14 November.
