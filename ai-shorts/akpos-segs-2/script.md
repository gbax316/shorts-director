## Calls I made (say if any is wrong)

- **The customer is a voice and a silhouette, never a locked character.** She needs a third voice: a Nigerian woman, picked by your ear from two or three auditions.
- **Line 7 names how the help is delivered** (live answers) rather than claiming "personal attention". This follows your Build With AI page rule.
- **The sign says what he will be doing, not "closed",** so it does not suggest the class takes two full days off work.
- **The counter stops at 3.** It is a gag, not a statistic, so no real number is claimed.
- **Line 7 names the dates,** so the episode stops being usable after 14 November.
