# The new builders

Lagos, after work. People who never studied code are building websites and apps by directing AI. The film asks who builds the internet now.

**A call you can overrule:** the narrator talks about you in the third person ("Orezi has built websites since..."). An AI voice should not speak as you in the first person. If you want the offer line in your own voice, record it and it drops straight into beat 6.
